**2. Key Indicators**

The key indicators that support the suspicion of a DoS attack are:

* Elevated ACK flag count (Feature 2): This suggests an established connection under high-rate interaction, consistent with a DoS attack.
* Low forward window bytes (Feature 3): This indicates an anomalous or absent forward window value, which can be indicative of scanning or attack tools.
* Absence of PSH flags (Feature 4): This suggests few PSH flags despite many packets, indicating payload-free flooding traffic.
