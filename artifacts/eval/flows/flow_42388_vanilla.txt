**5. Recommendations**

Based on the detection of this DDoS attack, the following recommendations are made:

* Implement rate limiting and traffic shaping policies to prevent excessive traffic from reaching the targeted web server.
* Configure the web server to use a Content Delivery Network (CDN) to distribute traffic and reduce the load on the server.
* Monitor network traffic and system logs for suspicious activity and implement incident response procedures to quickly respond to future attacks.
* Consider implementing a Web Application Firewall (WAF) to detect and prevent DDoS attacks at the application layer.
* Conduct a thorough investigation to identify the source of the attack and take appropriate measures to prevent future attacks.

By following these recommendations, the organization can reduce the risk of future DDoS attacks and ensure the availability and integrity of its network and systems.
