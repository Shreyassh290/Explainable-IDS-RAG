**Threat Assessment**
The detected DDoS attack is a type of Network Denial of Service (DoS) attack, which is a sub-technique of T1498.001 [1]. This type of attack is characterized by the direct sending of a high-volume of network traffic to a target, which can reduce the availability and functionality of the targeted system(s) and network. The attack can be performed by a single system or multiple systems spread across the internet, which is commonly referred to as a distributed DoS (DDoS).

**Recommendations**
Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are made:

* Implement IP address spoofing and botnet detection mechanisms to identify and block malicious traffic.
* Monitor network traffic and system resources to detect and respond to potential DoS attacks.
* Implement rate limiting and traffic shaping to prevent excessive network traffic.
* Conduct regular security audits and vulnerability assessments to identify and remediate potential vulnerabilities.

By implementing these recommendations, the organization can reduce the risk of successful DoS attacks and improve the overall security posture of its network and systems.
